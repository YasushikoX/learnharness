First build of this fork: LearnHarness changed for studying a university class from its own lecture PDFs. Based on LearnHarness 0.7.0 by way of Tobias Samsonsen's fork.

## What is in it

- **PDFs read whole.** Each PDF is converted to page-marked text by a converter you choose (built-in reader, a self-hosted Docling server, a Mistral OCR preset, or your own service described by a profile; "Draft a connector with AI" can write that profile), then indexed in full: summary, vocabulary with definitions and pages, concepts, figures. A class's documents form its glossary.
- **Vocabulary coverage.** Core terms are assigned to skills when you approve the class map; each lesson is told which terms it owes; the course page shows what was introduced and checked. Refusing a lesson's finish over unintroduced terms is an opt-in setting (Settings → General → Lessons), off by default.
- **Written answers by default**, graded against key points shown after you answer; harder follow-up questions; explanations one idea at a time; every figure with a "Look at" note and every demo with a "Try this".
- **Questions mode:** ask anything about a class and get a short, cited answer from its material; it does not count toward grades or mastery.
- **Reply reliability:** keepalive while the model thinks, Continue after a cut-off, Retry after a dropped connection, and a per-turn timing log.
- **Read-aloud** for questions, the **confidence slider**, and faster conversation switching.

The full list with the state of each change is in [FORK.md](https://github.com/YasushikoX/learnharness/blob/main/FORK.md).

## Installing

- **Windows:** run `LearnHarness-0.7.0-fork.1-x64-setup.exe`. The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen asks once ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- **macOS (Apple Silicon):** open `LearnHarness-0.7.0-fork.1-arm64.dmg` and drag the app to Applications. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized: the first time, right-click the app → Open. If macOS says the app is damaged, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LearnHarness.app`.
- It uses the same app name and data folder as the stock LearnHarness, so it replaces an installed stock version and opens its data.

## Updates

Later builds of this fork are published here. On Windows the app finds and installs them itself. On macOS an ad-hoc signed app cannot update itself: download the new DMG and replace the app.

## Known limits

- The new teaching prompts and the vocabulary tracking have had little real use; expect rough edges.
- The Mistral OCR preset and the AI-drafted connector have not been tried against real services.
- The macOS app uses the classic icon.
