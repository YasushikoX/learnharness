Second build of this fork: a full redesign of how the app looks. Nothing about teaching, grading or your data changes; everything in [build 1](https://github.com/YasushikoX/learnharness/releases/tag/v0.7.0-fork.1) is still here.

## What changed

- **New look, in the style of macOS and T3 Code.** The interface uses the system font (SF Pro on a Mac, Inter on Windows) instead of a coding font; lesson text stays in Source Sans 3, now at 17 px. One set of text sizes, one set of control heights and corner radii, and one family of buttons across the app, with one blue main action per screen.
- **New default themes.** Neutral **Dark** (the new default) and **Light**. The previous brown-and-gold look is kept as the **Warm Brass** theme (Settings → Appearance), and every other theme and colour override still works. Bold words are no longer link-blue in light themes, and diagrams follow the theme's colours.
- **Lessons.** Lesson text, figures and the question card share one centred reading column. The question card is a single card with the tools on the left and one Submit on the right; a short line says when Submit is waiting for your confidence. "Thinking while reading" is a small button instead of a second box. Your own labels and the tutor's are quieter.
- **Class page and Today.** "Next up" comes first with the one Start button; Learn, Ask and Practice sit in a slim row above it; the skill map, exam card and progress colours are calmer.
- **Sidebar, top bar and Settings.** A tidier sidebar with one search field and aligned rows, a slimmer top bar, and Settings rebuilt as grouped, aligned rows like macOS System Settings.
- **Keyboard focus is visible again**, and animations are shorter and calmer.
- **Transcript width:** unset now means the reading column; the slider goes from there up to 1920 px (the old "Full" stop is gone).

The redesign was scored by a four-model design council on screenshots of every screen: 4.2 / 10 before, 7.1 after the first pass, and 7.6 after the polish pass (from the two reviewers who still had quota left).

## Installing

- **Windows:** run `LearnHarness-0.7.0-fork.2-x64-setup.exe`, or let build 1 update itself. The installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen asks once ("More info" → "Run anyway").
- **macOS (Apple Silicon):** open `LearnHarness-0.7.0-fork.2-arm64.dmg` and drag the app to Applications, replacing the old one. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized: the first time, right-click the app → Open. If macOS says the app is damaged, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LearnHarness.app`.
- It uses the same app name and data folder as the stock LearnHarness and as build 1.

## Known limits

- In dark themes, generated figures keep their light background (use the figure's invert control).
- The new look has been checked on screenshots of every screen at desktop, 1024 px and phone widths, not yet on an Android device.
- Everything listed under "Known limits" for build 1 still applies.
