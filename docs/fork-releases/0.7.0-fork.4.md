Fourth build of this fork: small fixes and natural read-aloud voices (Google Gemini). Everything in [build 3](https://github.com/YasushikoX/learnharness/releases/tag/v0.7.0-fork.3) is still here.

## What changed

- **Natural read-aloud voices.** Questions can now be read with Google's Gemini voices instead of the computer's robotic ones. Settings → General → Read aloud → Voices: Google (natural, online). It needs a free Google AI Studio key: the same key as the Google AI Studio PDF converter, so if you already set that up (or a Google key under model providers) there is nothing to enter; otherwise paste one there and press Test. Choose Natural or Faster, and a voice for English and one for Norwegian, each with a ▶ sample. The speech rate still applies. Read-aloud audio is kept on this computer, so reading a question again costs nothing, and long questions start playing after the first sentences. The system voices take over whenever Google can't read (no key, offline, an error, or the free limit). See docs/read-aloud.md.

## Your text and Google's free tier

With Google voices on, the text read aloud (the question and its options) is sent to Google. On the free tier Google may use it to improve its products, and people may review it. The free tier has limits per minute and per day (the daily one resets at midnight Pacific time); when one is reached the system voices read, with a quiet notice once, until it resets. Choose System voices to keep everything on this computer.

## Known limits

- The Google voices were written from Google's documentation and tested against a simulated API only; Settings marks them unverified until a Test succeeds.
- Only questions are read aloud; the tutor's explanations are not yet.
- Everything listed under "Known limits" for build 3 still applies.
