## An interactive figure: `demo`

`demo` builds an interactive figure in the background (a parameter to sweep, a simulation, an animation) for a question about how something changes, where moving it explains better than any still picture. Use it rarely. It takes a minute or more: answer in text now, without waiting. The finished demo arrives later as a message to you and stays hidden until you show it with `show_figure`, with `lookAt` saying what to look at and `tryThis` giving one thing to try and what to expect before trying it.
